# Forest Post Café — Art Direction Bible

**Visual identity:** Classic Low Poly · Flat-Shaded Stylized 3D  
**Status:** Concept-stage guidelines, subject to iteration  
**Reference images provided in the design brief:** style inspiration only, not owned game concept art.

## 1. Visual north star
A miniature storybook landscape that feels crafted from approachable polygonal shapes: believable enough to inhabit, simplified enough to feel safe, and warmly lit without losing crisp silhouettes. Every scene must plausibly belong to the **same** game.

## 2. Shared palette
| Role | Hex | Suggested use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest green | `#496B59` | Deep firs, shadow-side foliage |
| Soft sage | `#8BA58A` | Meadow foliage, accent surfaces |
| Honey oak | `#B88254` | Café walls, porch, signposts |
| Terracotta | `#C87760` | Roof details, flower pots |
| Warm cream | `#F6E8D0` | Plaster, soft window highlights |
| Lake teal | `#8DC9CB` | Water, small painted details |
| Postal coral | `#C85E55` | The recognizable delivery bicycle |

Prefer calm color relationships; let warm light and restrained coral accents provide focal points. Rainy evenings may deepen values and desaturate surroundings while retaining the same materials.

## 3. Architectural canon
**Forest Café / post office**
- Small, friendly timber cottage with an adjoining post counter and coffee room.
- A steep **mossy sage-green gable roof**, subtle terracotta ridge caps, honey-oak timber siding, cream wall accents, and muted teal window trim.
- A wooden porch with simple balustrades, practical stairs and a compact seating area.
- Readable wooden mailbox and post sorting props; do not depend on legible tiny lettering.
- Warm, inviting windows; outdoor pots and geometric wildflowers.
- Maintain the same roof silhouette, porch location, door color, trim, and mailbox style between morning, night, exterior, and interior studies.

**Secondary buildings**
- Village cottages share the same geometry and material family while varying roof height, flower arrangements, and a few restrained accent colors.
- Bridges, signposts, and benches use visible clean wooden block construction.

## 4. Character and vehicle canon
- Postal worker: friendly, simplified adult proportions, approximately 3–4 heads tall; compact backpack or satchel, comfortable outdoor clothes; readable face without anime stylization.
- Bicycle: **coral-red frame**, simple thick tires and a wicker postal basket; recognizable in static and moving scenes.
- Cat: small, rounded low-poly silhouette with subdued colors.
- No extreme facial realism or disproportionate comic caricatures.

## 5. Geometry and materials
- Intentionally flat-shaded facets; faceted evergreen foliage with clear tiers and trunk shapes.
- Readable shapes at normal game camera distance.
- Matte, broad-color materials; limited procedural variation for depth.
- Avoid noisy normals, high-frequency textures, glossy plastics, realistic skin, or micro-details.
- Props should communicate function through silhouettes before detail.
- Favor geometric flowers, simple benches, chunky coffee cups, clear envelopes, and packages.

## 6. Lighting and atmosphere
**Morning:** warm, side-lit sun; long but soft shadows; cool background separation and slightly misty distance.  
**Midday/travel:** gentle, clear daylight; distinct paths and clean color reads.  
**Interior:** warm diffuse bounce, amber pendants, sun shafts through windows; comfortable exposure with distinct coffee and mail areas.  
**Rainy evening:** muted cool air, subtle wet-ground response, warm golden windows; avoid strong bloom or neon reflections.

Do not change the architecture or palette simply because the time of day changes.

## 7. Environmental canon
- Fir and spruce trees built from deliberate layered polygon masses.
- Calm grassy hills, gently winding sandy trails, sparse stones, small flowering patches.
- Water is a smooth stylized lake plane with restrained geometric highlight shapes.
- Distant mountains may be simplified blue-grey polygon silhouettes.
- Composition emphasizes a readable path, a story focal point, and inviting negative space.

## 8. Camera and composition
- Establishing art: cinematic three-quarter view with readable front door and winding path.
- Exterior detail: elevated three-quarter/isometric-like view; not forced orthographic if it harms proportions.
- Interior: cutaway / dollhouse view consistent with exterior dimensions.
- Travel: mid-distance three-quarter follow view, bicycle and route clearly visible.
- Village: wide landscape with a coherent bridge, cottages, and distant peaks.
- Rain: exterior viewpoint matched to the same cottage footprint and façade.

## 9. Image production rules
- Original art only; uploaded references must not be silently reused as produced in-game assets.
- No watermarks, readable text, fake UI, or logo overlays.
- A single unified style bible governs all six shots.
- Produce real PNG files at their requested exact dimensions.
- Validate image dimensions, PNG encoding, colors, composition, and filenames.
- Keep source generation prompts/version notes under `docs/art-prompts.md`.
- Add images to `assets/concept-art/` only when valid output exists; never include broken README references.

## 10. Quality acceptance checklist
- [ ] Same café roof / door / porch / window language across shots.
- [ ] Same coral-red bicycle and basket silhouette.
- [ ] Faceted low-poly trees, terrain, and buildings.
- [ ] Unified matte materials and pastel-inspired palette.
- [ ] Clear scene focal point and story mood.
- [ ] Appropriate morning/interior/day/evening light variations.
- [ ] No text, AI-like visual artifacts, excessive bloom, or style drift.
- [ ] Image physically opens; its dimensions match the delivery specification.

The guideline is intentionally engine-independent.
